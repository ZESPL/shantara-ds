# Glossary

Part of the [property handbook](../handbook.txt).

## Nursing care vs personal attendant

These are two separate services. Never merge them, and never describe one using the other's name.

### 24-hour nursing care
- What it is: Shantara's nursing team is on site 24 hours a day and looks after every guest in residence.
- Who gets it: every guest, automatically.
- Cost: included in the tariff. Never charged separately.
- How to get it: no request needed.

### Personal attendant (bystander)
- What it is: one attendant assigned to stay with one guest throughout their stay, for guests who need continuous one-to-one help.
- Who gets it: only guests whose doctor recommends it.
- How to get it: requested and approved during the preliminary consultation.
- Cost: ₹2,000 per day, charged in addition to the tariff.
- It does not replace the nursing team. Guests with an attendant still receive 24-hour nursing care.

### Naming rules
- Name of the paid service: "Personal attendant (bystander)". On second mention: "personal attendant".
- Never call the paid service: nursing care, nurse support, 24/7 nurse, nursing service, helper, helper policy, or private nurse.
- Never describe 24-hour nursing care as paid, optional, on request, or subject to doctor recommendation.
- The term "Helper Policy" is retired.
- Wherever one service is mentioned on a page that also mentions the other, include this line: "The personal attendant is in addition to the 24-hour nursing care every guest receives."
- In structured data, medicalSpecialty "Nursing" and the amenity "24-hour Nursing Care" refer only to the on-site nursing team. The personal attendant is never represented as nursing in schema.

## Devices and work

Shantara encourages guests to disconnect, because constant connection works against the healing process. Some working professionals cannot switch off completely, so Shantara provides one designated place for work.

### Where devices are allowed
- In the guest's own room.
- At the designated workstations.

### Where devices are not allowed
- Treatment areas, dining areas, the library, yoga areas and meditation areas.

### Naming rules
- Call them "designated workstations". Never "business centre", "co-working space" or "work lounge".
- Never describe Shantara as a workation, remote-work or digital-nomad destination.
- Whenever workstations are mentioned, frame them as the exception to the device policy, not as a feature. Use this line: "Devices are discouraged across the retreat. Guests who need to work can use their own room or the designated workstations."
- Wi-Fi is described as "high-speed Wi-Fi". Do not write "free Wi-Fi" until this glossary says it is free.
