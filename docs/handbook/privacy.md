# 8. Guest Privacy & Confidentiality

Part of the [property handbook](../handbook.txt).

Guests share sensitive personal and health information in order to receive care. Protecting that information is a condition of every guest's trust in Shantara, and it is every team member's responsibility, not only clinical staff.

### What counts as confidential

- A guest's identity, presence at the property, and dates of stay
- Any health condition, diagnosis, treatment, or medication a guest discloses
- Room numbers, itineraries, and any other personal details shared with staff

### Rules for staff

- Never discuss a guest's condition, treatment, or reason for being at Shantara with anyone outside the care team. This includes other guests, the guest's own family or friends unless the guest has authorised it, media, and personal social media.
- Never confirm or deny that a specific person is a guest at Shantara to an outside caller or visitor without verified authorisation from the guest or management.
- Do not photograph or record guests without their consent, and never share guest images or details on personal social media or messaging.
- Share guest information with colleagues only when it is operationally necessary, for example a therapist passing a dietary restriction to the kitchen.

### Why this matters

Guests come to Shantara to address health conditions many would not want discussed casually. A breach of this trust affects the guest directly, and it affects Shantara's credibility as a clinical facility.
