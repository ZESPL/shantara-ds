### Our story

P.A. Kareem has practised naturopathy for more than 33 years. In 2000 he founded Hygiene Nature Cure Hospital. Under his leadership it grew into a 66-bed hospital, certified under the Keralam Clinical Establishment Act, and has treated more than 25,000 patients. Shantara continues that practice. The team, the philosophy and the commitment are the same. The setting is a retreat on the hilltop above the Chennamangallur valley.

Publish this history only as the About story. On that page, say people rather than patients, and leave out the bed count and the certification. The year 2000 is the founding of Hygiene Nature Cure Hospital, within his longer practice. Shantara continues that practice.

### What "Shantara" means

Shantara means "A Star of Serenity."

Shantara is your guiding star, your North Star on the journey towards peace, balance, and wellbeing. It is a place to step away from the noise of everyday life, reconnect with yourself, and find your way back to a more balanced state of being.

### Our approach

Healing at Shantara begins by understanding what the body is asking for, rather than by "fixing what is broken." The approach follows naturopathy principles, in which nature leads and the body restores itself at its own pace. Each guest is treated as a whole, with physical health, mental well-being, and daily habits treated as connected. Treatment is unhurried and personalised, and it focuses on root cause rather than symptom relief.

Unlike hospitality-focused wellness resorts, Shantara operates as a clinical naturopathy retreat, where treatment efficacy depends on controlled conditions: prescribed diets, structured routines, minimal external disruption, and continuous monitoring. The house rules in [section 13](policies.md#section-13) exist to protect these clinical outcomes, not to restrict guests unnecessarily.

### Why Shantara, today

Modern illness is rarely sudden. It builds quietly through routine, stress, and neglect, and what it often needs is not intervention but interruption, meaning a pause and a space to reset. Shantara exists for that moment.
