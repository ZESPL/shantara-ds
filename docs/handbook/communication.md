# 15. Guest Communication Guide

Part of the [property handbook](../handbook.txt).

Do not quote the 30-second line on this page, or use “patients” / “wellness retreat”, as public product language.

### How to describe Shantara in 30 seconds

> Shantara is a naturopathy wellness retreat in Kozhikode, built on more than two decades of clinical naturopathy practice. Every guest starts with a doctor consultation, and their program of treatments, therapies, and diet is personalised from there. It's a place to slow down and let the body reset, guided by medical care rather than left to chance.

### Terms staff should use

"Doctor-guided," "personalised program," "root cause," and "naturopathy protocol"

### Terms and claims staff should avoid

- Avoid promising cures or specific medical outcomes, such as saying "this will cure your diabetes." Describe support and lifestyle correction instead of guarantees.
- Avoid calling it a "hospital stay" to guests booking a wellness or retreat program. Reserve hospital framing for genuinely clinical or medical-programme contexts.
- Avoid answering specific medical questions in detail. Redirect these to the doctor instead.
- Avoid confirming a booking for a guest describing a critical or acute condition, such as a recent heart attack, stroke, or active cancer treatment. Route these to a doctor for an eligibility check first, per [section 5](admission.md#section-5).

### Common guest misconceptions

- That it is a spa or resort with optional wellness add-ons. It is the reverse: wellness is the core, and hospitality supports it.
- That they may leave the property during the stay. They may not, except for a genuine medical emergency escorted by a designated team member.
- That a site-wide 7-night minimum applies to every stay. It does not. The minimum stay is 3 nights; beyond that, duration is advised following consultation, and most programmes are typically designed around a week or longer.

### When to refer a question elsewhere

- Anything about diagnosis, medication, or treatment specifics should go to the doctor.
- Anything about billing or cancellation exceptions should go to front office or management.
- Anything about corporate or group bookings, partnerships or contracting should go to the Head of Sales & Partnerships: +91 98955 95990, sales@shantara.life.
