### How the team is organised

| Division | Departments | Owner |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Hospitality | Front Office, Housekeeping, F&B Service, F&B Production, Maintenance | Operations Manager |
| Clinical | Consultation, Therapy, Nursing, Lab | Medical Director |
| Commercial & Administrative | Sales, Marketing, Finance, Human Resources | Chief Executive Officer |

### Who handles what

| Guest need | Who to involve |
| --- | --- |
| General health question, treatment plan | Doctor on duty (Clinical) |
| Emergency or medical concern | Doctor on duty, then nearest hospital |
| Diet or meal-plan question | Doctor and F&B Production (kitchen) |
| Yoga, physiotherapy, or therapy session | Assigned therapist (Clinical) |
| Booking, room, or stay-duration question | Front Office |
| Housekeeping or room maintenance issue | Housekeeping / Maintenance |
| Corporate or group booking, partnership or contracting enquiry | Head of Sales & Partnerships |
