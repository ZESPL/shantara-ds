# 6. Treatments & Therapies

Part of the [property handbook](../handbook.txt).

Shantara's therapies draw from naturopathy principles. They work with natural elements and the body's own rhythms rather than overpowering it. Every therapy below may form part of a guest's personalised program, and is prescribed by a doctor rather than requested a la carte.

| Therapy | What it is / what it's for |
| --- | --- |
| Mud Therapy | Mineral-rich earth application that calms inflammation, improves circulation, and reduces physical tension while grounding the mind. |
| Yoga Therapy | Gentle, mindful movement and breathwork adapted to individual needs, focused on posture, breathing, and inner stability rather than intensity. |
| Acupuncture | Fine needles used with precision to stimulate energy pathways, support circulation, and reduce pain. |
| Hijama (Cupping Therapy) | A traditional suction-based method to release stagnation, ease muscle tension, and support detoxification. It is delivered under medical supervision. |
| Physiotherapy | Guided exercises and hands-on therapy to rebuild strength, mobility, and posture. |
| Hydrotherapy | Water-based treatments at controlled temperatures that improve circulation, relax muscles, and support the body's natural healing. |
| Diet Therapy | Personalised nutrition based on each guest's condition and treatment goals, treating food as daily support for recovery rather than restriction. |
| Fasting Therapy | Medically supervised fasting that reduces digestive load to support detoxification and metabolic balance. It is gradual and monitored, and guests should never self-direct a fast. |
| Massage Therapy | Therapeutic touch that relaxes muscles, improves circulation, and reduces physical stress. |
| Heliotherapy | Controlled natural sunlight exposure that supports vitamin balance, mood, and immune function. |
| Herbal Medicine | Plant-based formulations selected for each individual's needs to complement other therapies. |
| Manual Therapy | Hands-on techniques that improve joint mobility, reduce stiffness, and relieve tension. |
| Mind-Body Therapy | Guided practices addressing the mind-body connection to reduce stress and support emotional balance. |
