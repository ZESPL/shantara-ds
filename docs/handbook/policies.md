# 13. Guest Policies & House Rules

Part of the [property handbook](../handbook.txt).

These policies protect the clinical integrity of naturopathy treatment and create an optimal healing environment. They are not arbitrary restrictions, and staff should be able to explain the reasoning behind each one if a guest asks.

### Cancellation policy

Cancellations must be made in writing, through the cancellation request form at `shantara.life/en/cancellation` or by email to `heal@shantara.life`. An email must give the guest's name, booking email and arrival date. The refund tier is set by the date and time (IST) the cancellation is received. Confirm every cancellation to the guest by email. A guest with no confirmation should call or WhatsApp +91 9553 600 100.

- More than 30 days before arrival: 100% refund
- 21 to 30 days before arrival: 50% refund
- 20 days or less before arrival, or no-show: no refund
- Early departure by choice: no refund for unused nights
- Early departure on medical grounds, certified by a Shantara doctor (emergency, hospital transfer, or doctor-advised stop): unused nights refunded in cash
- Admission declined by a Shantara doctor before arrival: 100% refund, whatever the date
- Admission declined by a Shantara doctor on arrival: full refund less the nights and services already used
- Disciplinary discharge of the guest or companion: no refund, including prepaid supplements

Refunds go to the original payment method, in the currency paid, within 7 working days of the confirmed cancellation. Bank and gateway charges and exchange-rate differences are not refunded.

Prepaid supplements (non-participating companion, extra bed or mattress) are refunded for unused nights, and prepaid additional therapies not given are refunded, however the stay is cancelled or ends, except after a disciplinary discharge. The cancellation tiers do not apply to them. Therapies already given are not refunded.

If Shantara cancels, or force majeure prevents the stay, offer a free date move or credit for a stay within 12 months. Refund in full if the guest cannot use either. Shantara is not liable for the guest's travel costs.

### Bookings made through an agent

- Bookings made through a Shantara agent follow this same policy.
- Refunds are paid by Shantara directly to the guest, never through the agent.
- Agents cannot approve refunds, exceptions or date changes on Shantara's behalf.

### Date changes

A stay can be postponed once at no charge, with written notice at least 21 days before arrival, subject to availability. The guest must give the new dates within 30 days, and the new stay must start within 6 months of the original arrival date. If the new rate is higher, the guest pays the difference. If it is lower, the difference is adjusted against other charges during the stay. After a date change, the booking cannot be changed again or refunded.

### Stay duration

The minimum stay for any programme is 3 nights. Most programmes are designed around a week or longer so naturopathy protocols have time to take effect; that is typical duration, not the minimum. The guest's doctor recommends the optimal programme duration at the preliminary consultation. Early departure by choice does not entitle a refund for unused nights of the booked stay; a doctor-certified medical departure does — see the cancellation policy above. Do not quote rates here; they live on the tariff surface.

### Non-participating companions

- A companion may accompany a guest who needs a caretaker, if this is requested and approved during the preliminary consultation.
- A companion sharing the guest's room pays the nightly companion supplement shown on the current tariff. It covers meals aligned with retreat guidelines.
- A companion staying in a separate room is charged separately. The team confirms the amount.
- An extra bed or mattress is charged per bed, per night, as shown on the current tariff.
- Companions must follow all retreat policies, including diet, movement restrictions, prohibited items, attire, and conduct. Violation results in discharge without refund, and companions may not bring outside food or leave the premises.

### Personal attendant (bystander) {#helper-policy}

Every guest receives 24-hour nursing care. It is included in the tariff. No request is needed.

If a guest's condition requires continuous assistance, Shantara assigns a personal attendant (bystander) for 24/7 support, for a daily charge that the team confirms. This must be requested during the preliminary consultation and is subject to the doctor's recommendation.

The personal attendant is in addition to the 24-hour nursing care every guest receives.

### Prohibited items

Alcohol, packed food, non-vegetarian food, non-pharmaceutical drugs, cigarettes, and any other outside food are not permitted anywhere on the premises. This rule is enforced strictly, and any violation may result in discharge without refund. Tea and coffee: [Food & Dining](food.md#tea-and-coffee).

### Movement during stay

Leaving the premises during the stay is not permitted. This restriction supports treatment efficacy and guest safety, since the body is in various stages of detoxification and healing that require continuous monitoring, a controlled diet, and a structured routine. In a genuine emergency requiring a guest to leave, they must be accompanied by a designated team member.

### Digital detox

Guests are strongly advised to minimise use of mobile phones, laptops, and other communication devices, to rest the mind and support healing. If use is necessary, it should be restricted to the guest's own room or the designated workstations so it does not disturb other guests. Devices are not permitted in treatment areas, dining areas, the library, yoga halls, meditation halls, or other shared therapeutic spaces. Devices are discouraged across the retreat. Guests who need to work can use their own room or the designated workstations.

### Retreat conduct

Guests are asked to follow their prescribed treatment schedule, respect quiet hours and shared spaces, converse in a low tone, and be considerate of other guests' healing journeys.

### Visitor policy

Guests are asked to avoid visitors during their stay, to preserve an undisturbed healing environment for all guests. Where a visit is necessary, visitors are allowed only from 17:00 to 18:00.

### Pet policy

Pets are not permitted, to maintain a controlled therapeutic environment.

### Smoking & substance policy

Shantara is a smoke-free, alcohol-free, and drug-free facility. Possession or use of tobacco, alcohol, recreational drugs, or e-cigarettes anywhere on the premises results in immediate discharge without refund.

### Treatment protocol disclaimer

All treatments and therapies follow the doctor's prescription, and package inclusions and exclusions are specified at the time of booking. Additional therapies and services are available at extra cost, only after doctor's approval. Packages cannot be customised, and the rate stays the same whatever the guest's food intake or treatment frequency. Individual results vary based on health condition and adherence to the program. Naturopathy is a complementary approach and not a substitute for emergency medical care.

### Post-stay support

Guests who complete a program remain part of the Shantara family, and doctors remain available through online follow-up appointments to discuss concerns and provide continued guidance as guests update the team on their progress.
