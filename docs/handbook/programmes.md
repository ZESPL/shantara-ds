# 9. Wellness & Medical Programmes

Part of the [property handbook](../handbook.txt).

Programmes bring together medical insight, naturopathic therapies, daily routines, and restorative care. Each targets a specific area of health while adapting to the individual.

Programme names are catalogue, not audience. Guests are matched after assessment. For need-led marketing groups and the many-to-many map from needs to programmes, see [ICP and marketing audience](../icp.md). That system must not be limited by the current list below.

### Stress Management

Helps the nervous system slow down and recalibrate. The goal is regulation rather than escape, restoring the body's natural ability to handle daily demands without staying in constant strain.

**Who it's suited for**

- Feel persistently overwhelmed or mentally fatigued
- Experience disturbed sleep or difficulty switching off
- Notice physical symptoms linked to prolonged stress
- Seek clarity, calm, and emotional balance

**What guests typically notice**

- Improved sleep quality
- Reduced mental restlessness
- Better emotional regulation
- Increased energy and clarity

### Detox

Supports the body's natural detoxification pathways by reducing burden rather than forcing elimination, so that internal systems regain clarity and balance.

**Who it's suited for**

- Feel persistently heavy, sluggish, or fatigued
- Experience digestive discomfort or irregular appetite
- Wish to reset after long periods of imbalance

**What guests typically notice**

- Sense of lightness and clarity
- Improved digestion and appetite awareness
- Better sleep and morning energy
- Reduced bloating

### Weight Management

Supports the body in finding its natural balance through nourishment, activity, and rest. The focus is on sustainable change rather than restriction or rapid results.

**Who it's suited for**

- Struggle with weight gain or difficulty managing weight
- Experience metabolic imbalance or low energy
- Have tried multiple approaches without lasting results

**What guests typically notice**

- Improved metabolic function
- Gradual, sustainable weight changes
- Better energy and stamina
- Improved digestion and sleep

### Diabetes Reversal

Supports the body in restoring blood-sugar regulation naturally through lifestyle correction and guided routines. The aim is long-term regulation rather than short-term control.

**Who it's suited for**

- Have been managing type 2 diabetes
- Experience fluctuating blood sugar levels
- Seek lifestyle-based support alongside medical guidance

**What guests typically notice**

- Improved blood sugar regulation
- Better energy levels and stamina
- Reduced dependence on external interventions

### Complete Healing

A deeper, more immersive program for guests managing multiple or long-standing concerns. It treats the body as a whole, addressing physical, mental, and metabolic health together through sustained naturopathic care.

**Who it's suited for**

- Are managing multiple or long-standing health concerns
- Seek a comprehensive, immersive healing experience
- Are ready to commit time to focused healing

**What guests typically notice**

- Improved overall energy and vitality
- Better balance across physical and mental health
- Greater clarity and emotional stability

### Weekend Rejuvenation

A short reset for guests with limited time, offering rest, relaxation, and gentle therapies without disruption to routine.

**Who it's suited for**

- Feel mentally or physically drained
- Have limited time for extended programs
- Seek a short wellness break

**What guests typically notice**

- Improved relaxation and mental clarity
- Better sleep quality
- Reduced fatigue and tension

### Executive Wellness

Structured, efficient care for guests in demanding professional roles, restoring balance without requiring them to disconnect from work responsibilities.

**Who it's suited for**

- Manage high-pressure professional roles
- Experience mental fatigue or burnout
- Have limited time for long wellness breaks

**What guests typically notice**

- Improved focus and mental clarity
- Better energy management through the day
- Reduced stress and physical fatigue

### Men's Vitality

Supports men’s energy, metabolic health, and hormone-related concerns through assessment-led routines, nourishment, activity, and rest. The programme is matched after clinical review; it is not a separate audience or a guarantee of any biomarker change.

**Who it's suited for**

- Notice low energy, vitality, or recovery that is affecting daily life
- Have hormone-related or metabolic concerns a doctor has named
- Want a structured residential stay rather than another short self-directed reset

**What guests typically notice**

- Better day-to-day energy and stamina
- Clearer sense of a sustainable routine
- Improved sleep and recovery when those were part of the plan

### Longevity

Supports guests who want to stay capable, independent, and well for as long as possible through preventive routines, metabolic and movement support, and doctor-guided reassessment. The programme is catalogue, not a longevity-medicine or sanctuary product.

**Who it's suited for**

- Are investing in preventive health and healthy ageing
- Want to maintain strength, mobility, and metabolic balance
- Prefer a doctor-led residential stay over spa anti-ageing or biohacking packages

**What guests typically notice**

- Better energy and physical confidence in daily activity
- Clearer habits around meals, movement, and rest
- A measurable reassessment of what to continue at home

### Corporate Retreats

Group programs for organisations that help teams slow down, reset, and return with renewed energy and cohesion. Therapies and schedule are curated based on group size and objectives.

**Who it's suited for**

- Organisations wanting to support employee well-being
- Teams seeking reset and stress reduction
- Groups looking for meaningful offsite experiences

**What guests typically notice**

- Improved morale and group energy
- Better communication and collaboration
- Renewed sense of connection and balance

### Medical programmes (condition-specific)

Alongside the wellness programmes above, Shantara runs condition-specific medical programmes across 11 categories. Staff do not need to memorise every condition. Knowing the categories is enough to answer "do you treat X" and to know when to bring in a doctor.

| Category | Example conditions |
| --- | --- |
| Metabolic & Lifestyle Health | Type 2 diabetes, obesity, fatty liver (NAFLD), prediabetes/insulin resistance, dyslipidemia |
| Inflammatory & Musculoskeletal Rehabilitation | Arthritis, gout, low back/neck pain, spondylosis, IVDP, fibromyalgia |
| Hormonal & Endocrine Balance | PCOS, thyroid dysfunction, menstrual irregularities, perimenopause, fertility-related hormonal imbalance |
| Gastrointestinal & Gut Health Restoration | IBS, gastritis, peptic ulcer, colitis, constipation, dyspepsia |
| Neurological & Stress-Related Conditions | Migraine, chronic headaches, burnout, chronic stress, insomnia, mild anxiety |
| Renal Health | Early-stage CKD, diabetic/hypertensive nephropathy, kidney stones, microalbuminuria, nephritis |
| Heart & Circulatory Health | Hypertension, stable coronary artery disease, cardiac rehab, post-MI recovery, post-angioplasty/CABG rehab |
| Respiratory & Immune Resilience | Asthma, chronic bronchitis, COPD, allergic rhinitis, sinusitis |
| Dermatological & Skin Health | Acne, dermatitis, psoriasis, eczema, hyperpigmentation |
| Reproductive & Fertility Optimization | Female/male infertility, PCOS, ovulatory dysfunction, irregular cycles, endometriosis |
| Hematologic & Nutritional Disorders | Anemia, B12/D deficiency, hypoproteinemia, micronutrient deficiencies |

Every guest in a medical programme is under a doctor's direct care. Staff should route condition-specific questions to the doctor rather than answering from this table alone. See [section 5](admission.md#section-5) for conditions Shantara cannot admit.
