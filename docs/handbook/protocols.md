# 7. Medical Protocols & Scope

Part of the [property handbook](../handbook.txt).

This section defines what non-medical staff can and cannot advise on, once a guest is already at Shantara. For admission eligibility and the pre-consultation process, see [section 5](admission.md#section-5).

### Medication policy

All current medications must be declared during the preliminary consultation, and again at admission. Self-medication during the stay is strictly prohibited. Any ongoing pharmaceutical treatment continues only under the supervision of the in-house doctors, because naturopathy treatments can interact with certain medications and the doctors need complete oversight of everything entering a guest's system.

### When staff must escalate to a doctor

- Any guest-reported symptom, pain, or discomfort during a therapy session
- Any request to stop, extend, or modify a fasting or therapy protocol
- Any guest expressing distress, dizziness, or an adverse reaction to treatment or diet
- Any guest requesting to leave the property during their stay, since this requires a doctor or designated team member escort

### Scope of non-medical staff

- Non-medical staff may describe therapies, programs, and the general philosophy of naturopathy in the terms provided in this handbook.
- Non-medical staff must never diagnose, promise a health outcome, advise on medication changes, or contradict a doctor's recommendation to a guest.
- When in doubt, the correct answer is to say: "Let me check with the doctor on that for you."

See [Guest Privacy & Confidentiality](privacy.md#section-8) for requirements around everything discussed in this section.
