### What guests should know before anything else

- Shantara is a naturopathy-based retreat rather than a medical hospital in the conventional sense. Treatment is drug-free, root-cause focused, and doctor-guided.
- Every guest journey begins with a doctor consultation, and treatments and diet are personalised to the individual rather than standardised.
- Most programmes are designed around a week or longer so naturopathy protocols have time to take effect. That is typical duration, not a site-wide mandatory minimum. Shorter stays may be offered where clinically and commercially appropriate. The doctor recommends duration at the preliminary consultation. See [Guest Policies & House Rules](policies.md#section-13) for cancellation and stay-duration terms.
- Guests may not leave the property once admitted, except for a genuine medical emergency escorted by a doctor or designated team member. This surprises first-time guests, so staff should mention it early rather than let a guest discover it mid-stay. See [section 13](policies.md#section-13).
- Meals are part of the treatment, not a restaurant menu. Guests should not expect an a-la-carte experience or request outside food.
