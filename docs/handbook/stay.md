# 12. Stay Essentials: Booking, Arrival & Attire

Part of the [property handbook](../handbook.txt).

### Booking & payment

- Booked 31 days or more before arrival: 30% at confirmation, and the remaining 70% is due 30 days before arrival.
- Booked 30 days or less before arrival: 100% at confirmation.
- Reservations are subject to room availability.
- Guests can be quoted and pay in any currency shown on the current tariff.
- Accepted payment methods: bank transfer, UPI, and credit/debit cards.
- Room assignment is based on availability at the time of booking; preferences are accommodated wherever possible.

### Pre-arrival requirements

- A completed medical screening questionnaire and relevant medical records, as requested by the doctors during the preliminary consultation (see [section 5](admission.md#section-5)).
- A valid photo ID: Passport, Aadhar Card, Driving License, or Voter ID. Foreign nationals must also submit passport and visa documents on arrival.
- Tea and coffee: [Food & Dining](food.md#tea-and-coffee).

### Check-in & check-out

- Check-in: 14:00 hrs. Check-out: 11:00 hrs. Guests are asked to have lunch before departure.

### Airport & railway transfer

- Complimentary shared transfer from Calicut International Airport (about 25 km, roughly 45 minutes) and Calicut Railway Station (about 30 km, roughly 1 hour).
- Guests should share their flight or train details at least 48 hours in advance.

### Attire & laundry

- Two sets of cotton pyjama suits are provided to every guest, and guests are asked to bring only basic personal clothing.
- Wearing the retreat-provided pyjamas is mandatory. It maintains uniformity and helps guests disconnect from daily life during the healing process.
- Daily laundry of retreat attire is complimentary. Personal clothing laundry is available on a chargeable basis.
