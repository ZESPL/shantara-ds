### Room types

Rooms are designed with restraint, so comfort feels subtle rather than excessive, in order to support rest. Some rooms in each category open to mountain views. Every category can be booked for single or double occupancy. Rates are on the current tariff; this handbook does not quote them.

| Category | Specification |
| --- | --- |
| Executive Suite | 530 sq. ft. suite with balcony |
| Junior Suite | 420 sq. ft. suite |
| Premium Room | 460 sq. ft. room with balcony |
| Superior Room | 300 sq. ft. room with balcony |
| Standard Room | 260 sq. ft. room |

**All room categories include**

- En-suite bathroom with rain shower
- Individually controlled central air-conditioning
- 10-inch orthopaedic mattress, king or twin options
- Electronic safe locker, high-speed Wi-Fi, and a 49-inch smart LED TV

**What a stay includes**

- Accommodation
- The programme and treatments prescribed by the doctor
- Daily doctor consultation
- Diagnostic tests, as advised by the doctor
- All therapeutic naturopathy meals
- Entertainment and activities
- Two complimentary recreational therapies per week
- Airport and railway transfers (see [section 12](stay.md#section-12))
- Attire provided for the stay, and its laundry

**What a stay does not include**

- Recreational therapies beyond the two complimentary per week
- Treatments beyond those prescribed by the doctor
- Personal laundry
