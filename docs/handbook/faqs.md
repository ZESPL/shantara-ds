# 14. Guest FAQs

Part of the [property handbook](../handbook.txt).

| Guest question | How to answer |
| --- | --- |
| Is this a hospital or a resort? | Both, in spirit. Shantara evolved from Hygiene Nature Cure Hospital, a 66-bed naturopathy hospital established in 2000 that has treated more than 25,000 patients, into an immersive retreat. Every stay is doctor-guided, but the setting is a retreat rather than a clinical ward. |
| How long do I need to stay? | Duration is advised following consultation. Most programmes are designed around a week or longer so protocols have time to take effect; shorter stays may be appropriate for some offers. Typical duration is a clinical recommendation, not a sales policy. |
| Can I choose my own meals? | Meals are personalised by the doctor based on your condition and program, so it is best to think of food as part of treatment rather than a restaurant menu. |
| Can I bring my own medication? | Yes, but it must be declared at the preliminary consultation and again at admission. Self-medication is not permitted during the stay; ongoing prescriptions continue only under the doctors' supervision. |
| Do you treat [specific condition]? | Check the medical programme categories in [section 9](programmes.md#section-9), and the admission criteria in [section 5](admission.md#section-5). If unsure, say "let me check with the doctor" rather than confirming or denying. |
| Can I step out during my stay? | No. Guests may not leave the property once admitted, except for a genuine medical emergency escorted by a doctor or team member. This maintains the controlled therapeutic environment naturopathy treatment depends on. |
| What if I need to leave early? | Per the cancellation policy, early departure does not entitle a refund for unused nights of the booked stay. |
| Is alcohol or smoking allowed? | No. Shantara is a smoke-free, alcohol-free, and drug-free facility, and any violation results in immediate discharge without refund. |
| Can family visit me during my stay? | No, visitors are not permitted once a guest is admitted, to preserve an undisturbed healing environment. A companion may stay with the guest if approved in advance as a non-participating companion. |
