### 3. Who was treated

**Found in handbook**

- Programme names are a catalogue. Guests are matched after assessment. The programme is personalised. It is not a fixed package the guest picks. `docs/handbook/programmes.md`, `docs/handbook/quick-reference.md`, `docs/handbook/therapies.md`
- Ten wellness programmes. `docs/handbook/programmes.md`
  - Stress Management. For persistent overwhelm, mental fatigue, disturbed sleep, difficulty switching off, physical symptoms of prolonged stress. Guests typically notice better sleep, less mental restlessness, better emotional regulation, more energy and clarity.
  - Detox. For feeling heavy, sluggish, or fatigued, digestive discomfort, irregular appetite, or a reset after a long imbalance. Guests typically notice lightness and clarity, better digestion and appetite awareness, better sleep and morning energy, less bloating.
  - Weight Management. For weight gain, difficulty managing weight, metabolic imbalance, low energy, or approaches that did not last. Guests typically notice improved metabolic function, gradual sustainable weight change, better energy and stamina, better digestion and sleep.
  - Diabetes Reversal. For people managing type 2 diabetes, fluctuating blood sugar, or lifestyle support alongside medical guidance. Guests typically notice improved blood sugar regulation, better energy and stamina, and reduced dependence on external interventions. The aim is long-term regulation.
  - Complete Healing. For several or long-standing concerns, treated together. Guests typically notice better energy and vitality, better physical and mental balance, greater clarity and emotional stability.
  - Weekend Rejuvenation. A short reset for limited time. Guests typically notice better relaxation and mental clarity, better sleep, less fatigue and tension.
  - Executive Wellness. For high-pressure work, mental fatigue, or burnout, without a full break from work. Guests typically notice better focus and mental clarity, better energy through the day, less stress and physical fatigue.
  - Men's Vitality. Energy, metabolic health, and hormone-related concerns a doctor has named. Matched after clinical review. Guests typically notice better day-to-day energy and stamina, a clearer sustainable routine, and better sleep and recovery when those were part of the plan.
  - Longevity. Preventive stay for strength, mobility, and metabolic balance. Catalogue only. It is not a longevity-medicine product. Guests typically notice better energy and physical confidence, clearer habits for meals, movement, and rest, and a measurable reassessment of what to continue at home.
  - Corporate Retreats. Group programmes. Therapies and schedule depend on group size and objectives. Organisations, teams, and offsights. Guests typically notice better morale and group energy, better communication and collaboration, and a renewed sense of connection and balance.
- Eleven condition-specific medical programme categories. Every guest in a medical programme is under a doctor's direct care. Examples, not a full list. `docs/handbook/programmes.md`
  - Metabolic and Lifestyle Health: type 2 diabetes, obesity, fatty liver (NAFLD), prediabetes and insulin resistance, dyslipidemia.
  - Inflammatory and Musculoskeletal Rehabilitation: arthritis, gout, low back and neck pain, spondylosis, IVDP, fibromyalgia.
  - Hormonal and Endocrine Balance: PCOS, thyroid dysfunction, menstrual irregularities, perimenopause, fertility-related hormonal imbalance.
  - Gastrointestinal and Gut Health Restoration: IBS, gastritis, peptic ulcer, colitis, constipation, dyspepsia.
  - Neurological and Stress-Related Conditions: migraine, chronic headaches, burnout, chronic stress, insomnia, mild anxiety.
  - Renal Health: early-stage CKD, diabetic or hypertensive nephropathy, kidney stones, microalbuminuria, nephritis.
  - Heart and Circulatory Health: hypertension, stable coronary artery disease, cardiac rehab, post-MI recovery, post-angioplasty or CABG rehab.
  - Respiratory and Immune Resilience: asthma, chronic bronchitis, COPD, allergic rhinitis, sinusitis.
  - Dermatological and Skin Health: acne, dermatitis, psoriasis, eczema, hyperpigmentation.
  - Reproductive and Fertility Optimization: female and male infertility, PCOS, ovulatory dysfunction, irregular cycles, endometriosis.
  - Hematologic and Nutritional Disorders: anemia, B12 or D deficiency, hypoproteinemia, micronutrient deficiencies.
- Clinical eligibility, separate from the catalogue. Shantara accepts chronic, lifestyle-driven conditions manageable with naturopathic treatment, including diabetes, hypertension, obesity, PCOD/PCOS, thyroid disorders, digestive issues, arthritis, and stress-related disorders. `docs/handbook/admission.md`
- People Shantara cannot admit: critical or acute conditions needing emergency care, intensive intervention, or 24/7 monitoring. Named examples: recent heart attacks, unstable angina, stroke, severe organ failure, active cancer under treatment, severe psychiatric conditions requiring hospitalisation. Doctors may decline admission if the condition needs more than naturopathic care. `docs/handbook/admission.md`

- Other stay roles that are not programme cohorts: a non-participating companion approved at the preliminary consultation; a qualified nurse for 24/7 help if the doctor recommends it and the guest needs continuous assistance. `docs/handbook/policies.md`
- Length of stay: most programmes are designed around a week or longer so protocols have time to work. That is typical, not a mandatory floor. Shorter stays may be offered where clinically and commercially appropriate. There is no site-wide 7-night minimum. The doctor recommends duration at the preliminary consultation. Weekend Rejuvenation is described as a short reset. No programme has a night count. `docs/handbook/policies.md`, `docs/handbook/quick-reference.md`, `docs/handbook/faqs.md`, `docs/handbook/communication.md`, `docs/handbook/programmes.md`

### 4. How outcomes are measured

**Found in handbook**

- Pre-arrival also requires a completed medical screening questionnaire and relevant medical records, as requested by the doctors during the preliminary consultation, plus photo ID. `docs/handbook/stay.md`
- All current medications are declared again at admission. Self-medication during the stay is prohibited. Ongoing pharmaceutical treatment continues only under the in-house doctors. `docs/handbook/protocols.md`, `docs/handbook/faqs.md`
- Guests are asked to stop tea and coffee 3 to 4 days before arrival. `docs/handbook/stay.md`
- A stay includes the programme and treatments the doctor prescribes, a daily doctor consultation, and diagnostic tests as advised by the doctor. `docs/handbook/property.md`
- The only timed clinical checkpoint in the day plan is the doctor visit from 12:00 PM to 01:00 PM. The doctor also prescribes which treatments fill the morning and afternoon slots, and the diet at each meal. `docs/handbook/stay.md`
- Clinical departments include Lab, Nursing, Consultation, and Therapy, owned by the Medical Director. `docs/handbook/contacts.md`
- The approach depends on prescribed diets, structured routines, little outside disruption, and continuous monitoring. The handbook does not define what is monitored. `docs/handbook/about.md`
- Longevity includes "a measurable reassessment of what to continue at home". The measures are not named. `docs/handbook/programmes.md`
- Diet is prescribed by the naturopathy doctor. In-room dining is only on a doctor's recommendation. `docs/handbook/food.md`
- After a completed programme, doctors remain available through online follow-up appointments, and guests update the team on their progress. What is written down in that follow-up is not described. `docs/handbook/policies.md`

### 6. Safety

**Found in handbook**

- Staff must escalate to a doctor when any of these happen. `docs/handbook/protocols.md`
  - A guest reports a symptom, pain, or discomfort during a therapy session.
  - A guest asks to stop, extend, or modify a fasting or therapy protocol.
  - A guest shows distress, dizziness, or an adverse reaction to treatment or diet.
  - A guest asks to leave the property. Leaving requires a doctor or a designated team member as escort.
- Fasting therapy is medically supervised, gradual, and monitored. Guests must not self-direct a fast. `docs/handbook/therapies.md`
- Hijama (cupping) is delivered under medical supervision. `docs/handbook/therapies.md`
- An emergency or medical concern goes to the doctor on duty, then the nearest hospital. Nearest hospital: KMCT Medical College Hospital, Manassery P.O., Mukkam, Kozhikode 673602. 24-hour casualty: +91 99952 20044. Hospital: 0495 229 3500. `docs/handbook/contacts.md`

- Early departure does not entitle a refund. Disciplinary discharge has no refund. Possession or use of tobacco, alcohol, recreational drugs, or e-cigarettes causes immediate discharge without refund. A companion who breaks the rules can be discharged without refund. Prohibited items (coffee, tea, alcohol, packed food, non-vegetarian food, non-pharmaceutical drugs, cigarettes, and other outside food) may result in discharge without refund. `docs/handbook/policies.md`
- Doctors may decline admission. Naturopathy is not a substitute for emergency medical care. `docs/handbook/admission.md`, `docs/handbook/policies.md`
- Recent heart attack, unstable angina, and stroke cannot be admitted. The heart programme category still lists cardiac rehab, post-MI recovery, and post-angioplasty or CABG rehab. `docs/handbook/admission.md`, `docs/handbook/programmes.md`

### Wording rules for a published report

**Found in handbook**

- Do not quote the 30-second "wellness retreat" line as public copy. Do not use "patients" or "wellness retreat" as public product language. `docs/handbook.txt`, `docs/handbook/communication.md`
- Terms staff should use: "Doctor-guided", "personalised program", "root cause", and "naturopathy protocol". `docs/handbook/communication.md`
- Do not promise cures or specific medical outcomes. The handbook's example to avoid is "this will cure your diabetes". Describe support and lifestyle correction. Do not give guarantees. `docs/handbook/communication.md`
- Do not call a wellness or retreat booking a "hospital stay". Hospital framing is reserved for genuinely clinical or medical-programme contexts. `docs/handbook/communication.md`
- Non-medical staff must never diagnose, promise a health outcome, advise on medication changes, or contradict a doctor. `docs/handbook/protocols.md`
- Individual results vary. Naturopathy is complementary and is not a substitute for emergency medical care. `docs/handbook/policies.md`
- Do not quote the book title "Drugless Cure for Diabetes and Hypertension" in public copy, because "cure" is an outcome claim. `docs/handbook/team.md`
- Do not quote cure or reversal claims from P.A. Kareem's lectures or from old headlines. A headline that states a cure or another outcome is rewritten as a plain description. `docs/handbook/team.md`
- Do not repeat the Onmanorama headline wording "diabetes reversal". `docs/handbook/team.md`
- Men's Vitality is not a guarantee of any biomarker change. Longevity is not a longevity-medicine or sanctuary product. `docs/handbook/programmes.md`
- Do not attribute a BNYS or a medical degree to P.A. Kareem. Use 33+ years, not "since 1989". Use 66-bed, not 160-bed. Use Founder, Chairman and Chief Naturopathy Consultant, not "Director and Chief Physician". Use Hygiene Nature Cure Hospital, not "Hygiene Nature Cure and Yoga". `docs/handbook/team.md`
- Never publish a registration number. Do not present Dr. Bahja Janu's Certified Professional Coder credential as a clinical qualification. Do not offer oncology nutrition as a service. `docs/handbook/team.md`
- Do not confirm that a named person was a guest. Do not publish identifying health details. `docs/handbook/privacy.md`
- Rates, currencies, and validity dates are not in the handbook and must not be added to this report. `docs/handbook.txt`
- Shantara is described as a clinical naturopathy retreat in the about section, and as a naturopathy-based retreat in the quick reference. Public pages still follow the wording bans above. `docs/handbook/about.md`, `docs/handbook/quick-reference.md`

**Gaps**

- A wording rule written for an efficacy report: Not found in handbook. The rules above still apply because the report will be published.

**Ask this person**

- Doctors, for any sentence that names an outcome, a medication change, or "reversal".
- Management, for final public wording. Marketing sits with the Chief Executive Officer. `docs/handbook/contacts.md`

## Measurement sheet draft

Every block below is a SUGGESTION for doctor review. The handbook does not say these measures are taken today, does not name who records them, and does not give a numeric cutoff. "Meaningful improvement" here means a doctor-reviewed definition, not a result. Suggested timing, unless a block says otherwise: the first doctor visit of the stay and the last doctor visit before departure. Suggested recorder: the doctor on duty. Lab is involved only if the doctor has already ordered a test. `docs/handbook/stay.md`, `docs/handbook/property.md`, `docs/handbook/contacts.md`
