### Financial capacity

{Approved} affordability overlay — **who can buy**, not a seventh ICP.

Every residential stay is treated as **HNI-affordable**. That is the capacity band for who can purchase a doctor-led multi-day stay. Campaigns still run as **ICP × need × geo × intent** among that capacity. Do **not**:

- Create an “HNI ICP”
- Buy Meta/Google “HNI” interest stacks as the strategy
- Hunt tulåh with sanctuary / WELL / longevity-medicine language (capacity ≠ that peer’s brand)
- Put HNI in public H1s unless research shows customers type it (they type the need)

Do not write income thresholds without research. Do not put rates in this document.
