### A. Definition

People whose work, lifestyle, or prolonged stress is affecting sleep, energy, mental wellbeing, or physical health, and who want a structured reset rather than a holiday they will spend answering email.

This ICP **includes** founders, CXOs, and other high-responsibility roles. It is **not limited** to them. Segments: entrepreneurs, senior executives, professionals, doctors, business owners, high-responsibility decision-makers, and other chronically stressed individuals.

### B. Core job to be done

Step out of an unsustainable pattern long enough to sleep, be assessed, and leave with a way of working and recovering that is not another collapse.

### C. Recognisable problems / needs

| Kind | Examples (not claims) |
| --- | --- |
| Symptoms | Insomnia, unrefreshing sleep, fatigue, irritability, racing thoughts, tension, headaches |
| Diagnosed conditions | Insomnia, burnout (as used in ordinary language or occupational health), mild anxiety, stress-related hypertension, migraine — only where a clinician has named them |
| Functional problems | Cannot switch off, declining work quality, relationship strain, reliance on stimulants or alcohol to cope, dread of the working week |
| Goals | Sleep through the night, feel capable again, reset before a breakdown, protect long-term health |

Do not position Shantara as a psychiatric hospital. Handbook excludes severe psychiatric conditions requiring hospitalisation.

### D. Trigger events

{Hypothesis}

- A period of not sleeping
- A panic or health scare attributed to stress
- Medical leave, or being told to “take time off”
- A board event, funding round, or clinical exam season ending
- A partner issuing an ultimatum
- A peer’s burnout or cardiac event
- Post-project exhaustion

### E. Desired outcomes

Guests typically **hope** for better sleep, a calmer nervous system, more energy, and distance from the pattern that brought them. Shantara does not guarantee restored performance, cured anxiety, or a transformed personality.

### F. Primary fears and objections

- “I cannot be unreachable.”
- “This will look like a holiday to my board / family.”
- Fear of being in a clinical environment, or the opposite: fear it is not clinical enough
- Privacy and being seen
- Whether devices and work are actually possible (Executive Wellness exists in the catalogue; do not invent a work policy)
- Mental-health stigma
- Time: a week feels too short, three weeks feels impossible

### G. Purchase criteria

Permission to rest without spa-frivolity; doctor involvement; typical duration; whether partial work access exists (only if Shantara confirms it); confidentiality; peer mix (they may not want a “CEO retreat” label in public); logistics.

| Role | How it often shows up {Hypothesis} |

| Guest | The exhausted person |
| Initiator | Guest, spouse, executive assistant, or occupational-health / HR in a corporate booking |
| Decision-maker | Guest; sometimes the organisation for Corporate Retreats (channel, not ICP) |
| Influencer | Peer founder, family doctor, coach |
| Companion | Sometimes a partner; often solo |

### I. Search and discovery behaviour

{Seed terms requiring research}

**Problem-led hypotheses:** burnout, can’t sleep, exhausted, chronic stress, always tired, work stress making me ill.

**Solution-led hypotheses:** burnout retreat, sleep retreat, stress management retreat, executive wellness, digital detox retreat (validate; may attract spa traffic).

**Question-led research:** how long to recover from burnout, can a retreat help insomnia, what happens in a stress management programme.

**Branded / comparison intent:** Shantara stress, Shantara executive; luxury wellness comparisons that may be poor fit.

Do not reduce this ICP to “founders in the GCC”. That is an overlay, not the definition.

### J. Search intent stages

Awareness (functioning is slipping) → problem research (burnout, insomnia) → solution research (holiday vs clinical reset vs sick leave) → comparison (spa vs retreat vs hospital) → commercial intent → brand validation (seriousness, privacy) → consultation → booking.

### K. Content requirements

Territories: what a structured reset actually contains; sleep and stress in plain language; who programmes are for (including but not only executives); privacy; a typical day; medication and mental-health limits. Do not glamorise overwork. Do not use “hustle then detox” as a brand story.

### L. Evidence required

Clinician review; protocol explanation; doctor credentials; facility capability; patient experience of rest and process; safety / eligibility including mental-health boundaries. Outcomes data if approved. No “we reverse burnout in 7 days”.

### M. Programme associations

Indicative: Stress Management; Executive Wellness; Neurological & Stress-Related Conditions; Weekend Rejuvenation as a possible short entry into a longer relationship. Corporate Retreats is a buying channel that may contain this ICP, not a separate ICP.

### N. Appropriate CTA

Book a Consultation; Speak to our team. Avoid “Book your reset now” with countdown timers.

### O. Conversion objective

Qualified enquiry. For this ICP, lead quality includes whether they understand it is a residential clinical stay, not a hotel weekend.

### P. Exclusions / poor fit

Marketing poor-fit: party weekends, spa-only, people seeking psychiatric crisis care, “workation” guests who will not participate. Handbook: severe psychiatric conditions requiring hospitalisation are not admitted. Do not invent further clinical mental-health exclusions here.
