## 3. Internal vs customer language

Internal names organise strategy, CRM, analytics, agency briefs, campaign structure, and reporting.

Customer language is what people type, say, and expect to read: Google Search, landing pages, articles, FAQs, ads, social, and enquiry conversations.

**Never assume an internal ICP name is language customers search for.** Agencies must validate actual search behaviour in each country and language. Internal labels are not automatically SEO keywords, ad headlines, or website headings.

| Internal (strategy) | Customer language (examples only) |
| --- | --- |
| Weight & Metabolic Health | lose weight, weight loss retreat, diabetes, high blood sugar, fatty liver, high cholesterol, metabolic health, obesity, lifestyle disease |
| Pain, Mobility & Rehabilitation | back pain, sciatica, arthritis, frozen shoulder, joint pain, rehab, move better |
| Stress, Sleep & Burnout | burnout, can’t sleep, exhausted, stress retreat, need a reset |
| Digestive, Inflammatory & Immune Health | IBS, bloating, acidity, gut health, inflammation, allergies |
| Hormonal & Vitality Health | PCOS, menopause, irregular periods, low energy, men’s health |
| Healthy Ageing, Prevention & Longevity | stay healthy as I age, preventive health, longevity, keep active |
| Short Health Reset | short wellness break, sleep reset, feel human again after travel |
