## 13. Short Health Reset

{Secondary} acquisition segment. **Not a seventh core ICP.** It must not define Shantara’s overall positioning.

### Motivations

Short wellness break; stress reset; sleep reset; post-travel fatigue; digestive overload; rejuvenation; preparation for an important event; trying Shantara before a longer residency.

### Strategy

Short stay → positive experience → ongoing relationship → assessment → possible future longer residency.

Measure this segment by **later qualified residencies**, not by making short stays the brand.

### Internal warning (not public copy)

Do not reposition Shantara as a spa, weekend getaway, beauty retreat, generic resort, or massage destination. That warning is for marketers. Public pages should describe the actual short stay, not argue “this is not a spa”.

### A–P (compressed, same template)

**A. Definition.** People who want a shorter, structured stay to reset or to try Shantara, without (yet) committing to a long residency.

**B. Job.** Recover enough function and trust to either return to life or return later for a longer programme.

**C. Problems.** Fatigue, poor sleep after travel, feeling “inflamed” after a period of excess, no time for a long stay. These are functional and situational more often than diagnostic.

**D. Triggers {Hypothesis}.** A gap in the calendar; post-event exhaustion; a friend’s stay; curiosity.

**E. Outcomes hoped for.** Feeling rested, sleeping better, a sense of the place. Not a completed metabolic or rehab result.

**F. Fears.** “A week is too short to be real”; “this might be a spa”; “I will be bored or over-treated”.

**G. Criteria.** Clarity of what a short stay includes; doctor contact; whether it can lead to a longer plan; logistics.

**H. Roles.** Usually the guest as initiator and decision-maker.

**I. Search {Seed terms requiring research}.** Weekend wellness, short detox, rejuvenation retreat, 7 day retreat. **High-contamination** segment: weekend wellness and rejuvenation mix heavily with spa and resort intent. Negative keywords will be essential. See [Operational paid exclusions](overlays.md#operational-paid-exclusions).

**J. Intent.** Often jumps from awareness to commercial short-break intent. Still route through consultation rather than instant self-booking if assessment is required.

**K. Content.** What a short stay is for; what it cannot do; how to continue afterwards. Do not let this content outrank core ICP education.

**L. Evidence.** Honest duration and inclusions; doctor involvement; guest experience. Do not use short-stay testimonials as proof of disease change.

**M. Programmes.** Weekend Rejuvenation; sometimes Executive Wellness; shorter Detox only if clinically and commercially real.

**N. CTA.** Book a Consultation; Learn about the programme. Not “Book this weekend”.

**O. Conversion.** Enquiry, then a stay that is correctly scoped, then CRM follow-up for a later core-ICP residency.

**P. Poor fit.** Spa shoppers, stag/hen weekends, beauty packages, guests who need a long medical programme but are being undersold a short reset.
