## 6. Profile template

Every core ICP uses this structure. The catalog wraps each profile in a reusable `.ds-icp-profile` block (`docs/doc.js`, `docs/doc.css`). Do not invent extra lettered sections. Do not skip letters.

| Key | Section | What belongs here |
| --- | --- | --- |
| A | Definition | Need, in internal language. One paragraph. |
| B | Core job to be done | The job they are hiring a residential stay to do. |
| C | Recognisable problems / needs | Separate **symptoms**, **diagnosed conditions**, **functional problems**, and **goals**. Diagnoses are not marketing claims. |
| D | Trigger events | Why now. {Hypothesis} until supported by enquiry or guest data. |
| E | Desired outcomes | What they hope for. No guaranteed outcomes. |
| F | Primary fears and objections | Only those typical of this ICP. |
| G | Purchase criteria | What they weigh when choosing a stay. No rates. |
| H | Buying roles | Guest, Initiator, Decision-maker, Influencer, Companion. |
| I | Search and discovery behaviour | Problem-led, solution-led, question-led, branded / comparison. Examples are {Seed terms requiring research}. |
| J | Search intent stages | Awareness → problem research → solution research → comparison → high commercial intent → brand validation → consultation intent → booking intent. |
| K | Content requirements | Territories, not a calendar. |
| L | Evidence required | From the evidence classes below. |
| M | Programme associations | Indicative. Not a restriction. Catalogue may change. |
| N | Appropriate CTA | Stage-appropriate. Do not force “Book now” on every surface. |
| O | Conversion objective | The success event for this ICP, usually a qualified consultation rather than an instant booking. |
| P | Exclusions / poor fit | Marketing poor-fit plus handbook eligibility. Do not invent clinical exclusions. |

**Evidence classes** (reuse these labels in briefs): clinician review; doctor credentials; original Shantara data; annual outcomes data; peer-reviewed external evidence; treatment / protocol explanation; facility capability; patient experience; safety / eligibility criteria.

**Agency working areas** (required at the end of every profile, including Short Health Reset): Market Research; Priority Countries; Search Demand; Validated Customer Language; Keyword Clusters; Competitor / SERP Findings; Recommended Landing Pages; Paid Campaign Opportunities; Content Opportunities; Negative Keywords / Poor-fit Traffic; Evidence Gaps; Performance Findings.

Each working area starts as {Not researched} / “To be researched/updated”. Status may move to {In progress}, {Validated}, or {Not applicable}. Do not fill Search Demand with invented numbers. {Not applicable} is for closed non-applicable rows (for example Performance / Search Console when no live property exists), not a substitute for inventing demand.
