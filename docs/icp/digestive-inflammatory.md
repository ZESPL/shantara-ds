### A. Definition

People with persistent digestive, inflammatory, immune, or related symptoms that affect quality of life, seeking a residential, doctor-guided period of assessment and support.

**Do not imply that all such conditions can be treated or cured.** Clinical suitability and [claim governance](claim-governance.md) apply to every sentence in this ICP.

### B. Core job to be done

Get a supervised reset of meals, rest, and routines while a doctor looks at digestion and inflammation in context, without promising that a named disease will disappear.

### C. Recognisable problems / needs

| Kind | Examples (not claims) |
| --- | --- |
| Symptoms | Bloating, acidity, irregular bowel habit, abdominal discomfort, fatigue with meals, skin flares, seasonal breathing difficulty |
| Diagnosed conditions | IBS, gastritis, colitis, constipation, dyspepsia, inflammatory arthritis, stable autoimmune conditions where a Shantara doctor considers a stay appropriate, allergic rhinitis, asthma or sinusitis where relevant |
| Functional problems | Avoiding food and travel, unpredictable days, embarrassment, energy that crashes after eating |
| Goals | More comfortable digestion, fewer flares, understanding of food triggers, a calmer inflammatory picture |

Naming a condition in this table does not mean Shantara markets a cure for it.

### D. Trigger events

{Hypothesis}

- Months of bloating or acidity that investigations have not settled
- A new autoimmune or inflammatory label and a wish to work on lifestyle
- A flare after a period of travel or festival eating
- Medication side-effects they want reviewed (clinical, not a marketing hook)
- A gastroenterologist suggesting lifestyle change they cannot implement at home

### E. Desired outcomes

Guests typically **hope** for less bloating and pain, more predictable digestion, and fewer inflammatory flares. No guaranteed remission. No “heal your gut in 14 days”. No claim that medicine was only managing symptoms.

### F. Primary fears and objections

- Fear of being one more person told “it’s just IBS”
- Fear of fasting or unfamiliar food
- Whether flares will be managed if they worsen on site
- Autoimmune guests fearing they will be promised a cure, or fearing they will be turned away
- Interaction with existing medication
- Credibility of naturopathy for IBD-spectrum or autoimmune disease

### G. Purchase criteria

Doctor oversight of meals and fasting; honesty about scope; what happens if symptoms worsen; typical duration; evidence and medical review of content; eligibility.

### I. Search and discovery behaviour

{Seed terms requiring research}

**Problem-led hypotheses:** IBS, bloating, acidity, constipation, gut health, inflammation, autoimmune, sinusitis, arthritis inflammation.

**Solution-led hypotheses:** gut health retreat, IBS retreat, naturopathy for acidity, fasting for digestion (handle with care; fasting is doctor-directed).

**Question-led research:** can a retreat help IBS, is fasting safe with gastritis, naturopathy and autoimmune disease.

**Branded / comparison intent:** Shantara digestion, Shantara detox; “detox” queries will mix clinical and spa intent. Negative keywords will matter.

### J. Search intent stages

Awareness → symptom and condition research → solution research (diet, supplements, retreats, hospitals) → comparison → commercial intent → brand validation (safety, doctors) → consultation → booking.

### K. Content requirements

Territories: how meals are planned; what supervised fasting is and is not; IBS in plain language; inflammation without miracle claims; eligibility; when to stay with conventional care. Detox language must not become a toxin-purge story. Separate Detox as a programme name from this ICP’s need.

### L. Evidence required

Clinician review; protocol explanation (especially fasting and diet); safety / eligibility; doctor credentials; external evidence for any physiological claim; patient experience of process, not of curing IBD or autoimmunity. Original data only if approved.

### M. Programme associations

Indicative: Gastrointestinal & Gut Health Restoration; Detox; Inflammatory & Musculoskeletal Rehabilitation; Respiratory & Immune Resilience. Autoimmune-related support is a capability to describe only where clinically appropriate, not a licence to advertise cures.

### N. Appropriate CTA

Learn about the programme; Book a Consultation; Meet our doctors. Never “Reverse autoimmunity”.

### O. Conversion objective

Qualified enquiry that can be clinically screened. High risk of poor-fit if ads over-claim.

### P. Exclusions / poor fit

Marketing poor-fit: juice-cleanse tourists, “cure my autoimmune disease” shoppers, unsupervised fasting enthusiasts. Handbook exclusions for acute / critical disease apply. Do not write a marketing list of untreatable GI diagnoses without a doctor’s source. When unsure, the public line is that a doctor reviews whether a stay is appropriate.
