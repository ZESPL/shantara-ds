## 12. ICP 06 Healthy Ageing, Prevention & Longevity

| Field | Value |
| --- | --- |
| ID | ICP 06 |
| CRM key | `healthy_ageing_longevity` |

| Internal thought | “I want to stay healthy and capable for as long as possible.” Not public copy. |

### A. Definition

People proactively investing in maintaining health, physical function, independence, and long-term wellbeing.

Two need clusters, **one ICP**:

- **Prevention / optimisation:** still functioning well; preventive health, longevity interest, physical performance, metabolic prevention, health screening, sustainable lifestyle improvement.
- **Healthy ageing:** older adults; mobility, strength, balance, metabolic health, independence, cardiovascular health, quality of life.

Do not create two top-level ICPs until data shows they behave as different markets.

### B. Core job to be done

Use a residential, doctor-guided stay as a concentrated investment in remaining capable, not as a response to a single emergency diagnosis (those cases usually arrive via another ICP).

### C. Recognisable problems / needs

| Kind | Examples (not claims) |
| --- | --- |
| Symptoms | Gradual stiffness, reduced stamina, milder metabolic drift, recovery that takes longer |
| Diagnosed conditions | Stable hypertension, prior cardiac event now medically stable, osteopenia mentioned by their clinician, type 2 diabetes already managed — often overlapping other ICPs |
| Functional problems | Fear of losing independence, wanting to travel and play with grandchildren, athletic performance plateau |
| Goals | Stay strong and steady, understand their numbers, build a sustainable routine, age well |

Cardiac recovery for **medically stable** guests is a handbook capability, not a promise to treat heart blockage. See claim governance.

### D. Trigger events

{Hypothesis}

- Retirement or pre-retirement
- A parent’s decline that they do not want to repeat
- A stable post-procedure window (angioplasty, CABG) where a doctor has said a stay may be considered
- A longevity article, screening package, or peer stay
- A milestone birthday
- Adult children encouraging a preventive stay

### E. Desired outcomes

Guests typically **hope** to stay mobile, independent, and metabolically steadier, and to understand their health more clearly. No guaranteed biological-age reduction. No guaranteed lifespan claim.

### F. Primary fears and objections

- “Am I well enough to come?” / “Am I too well to need this?”
- Fear of being in a sick-role environment
- Accessibility and safety
- Scepticism of “longevity” as a luxury fad
- Family logistics for older guests
- Whether this is medically supervised enough for a cardiac history

### G. Purchase criteria

Clinical governance; safety; accessibility; typical duration; what screening or review is actually offered (do not invent tests); companion policy; credibility versus spa longevity brands.

### H. Buying roles

| Role | How it often shows up {Hypothesis} |
| --- | --- |
| Guest | The person investing in their future health, often 50+ but not defined by age |
| Initiator | Frequently an adult child for older parents; the guest themselves for prevention / optimisation |
| Decision-maker | Shared in family-supported travel; the guest for self-funded prevention |
| Influencer | Family physician, adult children, peers |
| Companion | Common for older guests |

Age is an overlay and a handbook eligibility constraint (programmes: 18–80 in the current handbook). Age is not this ICP’s definition.

### I. Search and discovery behaviour

{Seed terms requiring research}

**Problem-led hypotheses:** healthy ageing, staying active after 60, prevent diabetes, heart health after stent (careful, clinical).

**Solution-led hypotheses:** longevity retreat, healthy ageing retreat, preventive health retreat, cardiac rehab retreat for stable guests.

**Question-led research:** how to stay independent as I age, is a naturopathy stay useful after bypass, what is a longevity programme actually.

**Branded / comparison intent:** Shantara ageing, Shantara longevity; Western longevity-clinic SERPs may not match Shantara’s offer. Do not imitate their claims.

**Copy risk (local competitive overlay):** Do not hunt affluent “clinical wellness sanctuary” demand with sanctuary, WELL, or longevity-medicine language. That frame belongs to peers such as tulåh (Calicut), not to Shantara’s ICP 06 public voice. See [Competitive set](overlays.md#competitive-set-overlays-not-icps).

### J. Search intent stages

Awareness (future health) → problem research (ageing, prevention) → solution research → comparison (clinic vs retreat) → commercial intent (often slower) → brand validation → consultation → booking.

### K. Content requirements

Territories: what healthy ageing means at Shantara; mobility and strength; metabolic prevention; medically stable cardiac recovery as eligibility-gated content; screening honesty; companion stays. Do not borrow Silicon Valley longevity slogans.

### L. Evidence required

Clinician review; doctor credentials; facility capability; safety / eligibility (especially cardiac); protocol explanation; external evidence; original data if approved. Guest experience of function and stay. No biological-age marketing without separate approval.

### M. Programme associations

Indicative: Longevity (named handbook wellness programme); Complete Healing; Weight Management; Detox; Heart & Circulatory Health for medically stable guests. Longevity is a programme name, not a seventh ICP — ICP 06 still owns the need. Do not market it as sanctuary / longevity-medicine / biohacking.

### N. Appropriate CTA

Book a Consultation; Meet our doctors; Read what to expect. Avoid “Add years to your life”.

### O. Conversion objective

Qualified enquiry. Family-initiated leads should still identify the guest and the initiator separately in CRM.

### P. Exclusions / poor fit

Marketing poor-fit: biohacking tourists expecting gadget protocols Shantara does not run; unstable cardiac or acute medical needs; spa anti-ageing. Handbook: above-80 programme exclusion; critical cardiac events. Do not advertise to recent heart-attack patients.
