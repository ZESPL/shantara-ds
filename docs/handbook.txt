**Staff extract — not public copy.** Use for property facts, policies, and `[TO CONFIRM]` flags. Prefer `content/` for entity records on the website. Do not quote §15 (the “wellness retreat” 30-second line) or use “patients” / “wellness retreat” as public product language. Rates stay on the tariff surface only, except the personal attendant charge in the [glossary](handbook/glossary.md).

This handbook exists so that any team member, regardless of department, can confidently answer the questions guests actually ask. Guests commonly ask what this place is, who the doctor is, what they can do during their stay, and what their package includes. This handbook is not an SOP manual, and it does not replace your department's operating procedures.

Read [Quick Reference](handbook/quick-reference.md) first. It answers between 80 and 90 percent of ordinary guest questions on its own. Then open only the section the question needs.

Before writing about nursing, nurses, attendants, bystanders or helpers, read the [glossary](handbook/glossary.md) and follow the naming rules exactly.

Before writing about devices, phones, Wi-Fi, work, workstations or amenities, read the [glossary](handbook/glossary.md). Never add an amenity that is not in the handbook amenities section.

Before writing a doctor credential, licence or membership, read the [glossary](handbook/glossary.md). A doctor who holds a BNYS, and who is legally entitled to the prefix "Dr.", is written "Dr. [Full Name], BNYS" or "Dr. [Full Name]".

[TO CONFIRM] Sections marked like this need input from Azhar, P.A. Kareem, or Dr. Bahja Janu before this handbook is finalised and printed.

This handbook is evergreen. It does not carry room rates, stay currencies or validity dates. The personal attendant charge is stated once, in the [glossary](handbook/glossary.md), so that service is never described as nursing care. Do not copy that figure onto any other page. Room rates are published only on the current tariff.
