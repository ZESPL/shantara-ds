Internal source of truth for who Shantara is trying to reach, why they look, and how teams should organise work around that.

This document is **not** a public webpage, **not** a set of marketing personas, and **not** permission to copy its internal labels onto the website, ads, or FAQs.

Hand it to SEO, performance marketing, social and content, PR, internal marketing, website and content, and CRM and analytics. Each team still has to validate the parts that affect their channel. See [What a marketing agency must do](icp/operating-guide.md#section-16).

Public language remains governed by [skill-copy.md](../ui_kits/website/skill-copy.md). Clinical eligibility remains governed by the [Property handbook](handbook.html). Programme names remain catalogue, not audience.

**Document status:** {Provisional} working system. Profiles are {Hypothesis} until an agency or Shantara validates them per country.

## Rules

- One source. Do not duplicate these ICP definitions in briefs, decks, or other docs. Link here.
- Assumptions are labelled. {Hypothesis} means Shantara has not established it with research or customer data.
- Do not invent search volume, CPC, competition, conversion rate, or market demand to fill a cell.
- Do not put rates, currency amounts, or "from …" figures here. Point at the tariff card.
- Internal warnings such as "not a spa" stay internal to this system. They must not become visitor-facing sentences. See [skill-copy.md](../ui_kits/website/skill-copy.md).
- Do not reorder ICP 01–06 cores without comparable measured stay demand across cores.
- Do not add a wellness traveller or "international" ICP. Geography remains an [overlay](icp/overlays.md). Financial capacity is an Approved HNI affordability overlay (who can buy), not a seventh ICP. See [Financial capacity](icp/overlays.md#financial-capacity).

## Organising principle

ICPs are organised around **recognisable customer needs and purchase intent**, not around programme names. Discovery and purchase follow need → content → consultation → assessment → personalised programme. Programme matching is **many-to-many**; do not rebuild the ICP list around today's catalogue.
