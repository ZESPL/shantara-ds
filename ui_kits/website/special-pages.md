# Special pages

Back to the [website skill](SKILL.md).

Special pages are public URLs outside the guest journey. Each one is indexable and listed in the sitemap for every locale where it is published. None of them appear in the navigation, the footer, or on guest pages. The address is sent directly.

Do not build a kit sample or a mockup. The page is specified here. Production uses the locale-prefixed URL. English is the source. Visitor-facing strings go through the locale dictionary. Do not translate them in this kit. The same English slug is reused under each published locale prefix.
