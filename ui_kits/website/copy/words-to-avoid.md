# Words to avoid

## Banned as product or hero language

Do not use these as product type, H1, title fragment, or primary CTA:

* Sanctuary
* Clinical wellness
* Nature cure (except accurate historical parent naming on About, e.g. Hygiene Nature Cure Hospital)
* Begin your wellness journey
* Wellness retreat
* Package, deal, offer (a rate sheet is a tariff; the action is "Make an Inquiry")

Internal negatives (“not a spa”, “not a hospital”, “not an Ayurveda resort”, “not a US naturopathic office”, “not Hygiene Nature Cure Hospital”) and the clinical-hotel model live in this skill, ICP, ad exclusions, and brand cards. They are not visitor-facing argument copy.

---

## Avoid generic luxury language

Shantara is a retreat with hospitality standards.

That does not require conventional luxury-hotel language.

Avoid phrases such as:

* Unparalleled luxury
* World-class luxury
* Opulent rooms
* Exquisite accommodation
* Indulgent experiences
* Lavish comfort
* Ultimate escape
* Luxurious sanctuary
* Sanctuary (as a product label)
* Clinical wellness
* Bespoke experience

Describe the actual accommodation and experience instead.

For example:

> Rooms are designed for comfortable residential stays, with quiet interiors and easy access to the retreat's treatment and common areas.

---

## Avoid mystical and spiritual claims

Do not write Shantara as if it promises spiritual transformation.

Avoid:

* Awaken your inner self
* Restore your energy
* Realign your body
* Cleanse your energy
* Unlock your body's wisdom
* Return to your true self
* Heal from within
* Reset your life
* Transform your being

Unless Shantara explicitly offers a spiritual practice and the copy accurately describes that practice, keep the language grounded.

## Be specific

Do not allow a vague word to carry the meaning.

These are hard bans in public marketing copy, including headings, leads, buttons, tiles and captions. They are not a caution list. Take the word out and write the fact, or cut the sentence.

* Luxury, luxurious
* Journey
* Transformative, transform
* Holistic
* Rejuvenate, rejuvenating
* Unwind
* Pamper
* Nestled
* Curated
* Bespoke
* Tailored
* Seamless
* Elevate
* Package, deal, offer
* Unparalleled, world-class, exquisite, opulent, lavish, indulgent, ultimate

Also banned as public product language, as before: sanctuary, nature cure (except the parent hospital's name), wellness journey, acreage, patients, wellness retreat.

**Wellness** on its own is not the product type. It must not replace "naturopathy retreat" in a hero, a title or schema. A discovery gloss in some markets is the only remaining use, and only in [voice and tone](voice-and-tone.md).

**You** and **your** on a fact are banned in the same way. Second person is for an instruction the reader carries out. See [voice and tone](voice-and-tone.md).

Restorative, natural, balance, healing and reconnection still cannot substitute for information. Do not use them as the heading or the point of a section.

Bad:

> Experience a transformative journey towards holistic wellbeing.

Better:

> A doctor plans the programme after assessment, from the health history and the goals the guest has stated.

Bad:

> Reconnect with your body's natural ability to heal.

Better:

> A stay may combine doctor-led naturopathy, nutrition, physical activity, therapies and rest.

## Never write

These are hard bans. They are wrong regardless of context, register or page. The marketing bans in the section above apply to public copy. The list below also applies inside the handbook when the text is published, and to any page.

* **“Four acres” / “4 acres” / “four hilltop acres”.** “Hilltop” alone is fine. See [naming and NAP](naming-and-nap.md#site-hilltop-not-acreage).
* **“Shantara Naturopathy”** as the business name, without “Retreat”. See [naming and NAP](naming-and-nap.md#business-name-nap).
* Any phone number other than **+91 9553 600 100**. See [naming and NAP](naming-and-nap.md#one-contact-number).
* **Patients**, **clients**, **customers** or **wellness retreat** as public product language (see [AGENTS.md](../../../AGENTS.md)). Policy pages may address the reader as "you".
* **Nursing care, nurse support, 24/7 nurse, nursing service, helper, helper policy, or private nurse** as the name of the paid one-to-one service. That service is the personal attendant (bystander). Included care is 24-hour nursing care. Before writing about nursing, nurses, attendants, bystanders or helpers, read [`docs/handbook/glossary.md`](../../../docs/handbook/glossary.md) and follow the naming rules exactly.
* An eyebrow or overline above a section or title, anywhere. See [voice and tone](voice-and-tone.md#punctuation).
