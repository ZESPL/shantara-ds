# Localisation

English is the published source. Planned localisation order is `en ar de fr ru hi ml` (see [`docs/icp.md`](../../../docs/icp.md) and [SKILL.md §15](../SKILL.md#section-15)). Do not invent Arabic, German, French, Russian, Hindi, or Malayalam public copy in this kit. Do not machine-translate health content and present it as authoritative. UI chrome may use the catalog Arabic fixture under `dir=rtl` for visual QA only. That is not a published translation. Italian and Spanish are not current marketing-localisation priorities.

When a localisation project writes real copy, it follows this skill in that language: clarity first, warmth second, brand expression third. A translation must not strengthen or weaken a clinical claim. Locale is not currency.
