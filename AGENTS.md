# Agent index — Shantara Design System

Read this before inventing copy, programmes, rates, or IA.

1. **Skills.** Read root [`SKILL.md`](SKILL.md). For the public marketing website, read [`ui_kits/website/SKILL.md`](ui_kits/website/SKILL.md) next; it lists the sibling files, and you open only the one your task needs (copy, stack, folder structure, images, share images, icons, video, premium design, IA, sections, content, technical, QA). For video, read [`ui_kits/website/skill-video.md`](ui_kits/website/skill-video.md): Cloudflare R2 + CDN, native `<video>` only, never commit a video file, never embed YouTube, Vimeo, Cloudflare Stream or Mux. For any presentation or deck (PowerPoint, Google Slides, Keynote, Canva, HTML, PDF): [`.cursor/skills/shantara-premium-presentations/SKILL.md`](.cursor/skills/shantara-premium-presentations/SKILL.md). The HTML brand-deck template is [`templates/brand-deck/`](templates/brand-deck/). For a design review or audit of components, sections or screens, follow [`.cursor/skills/apple-design/SKILL.md`](.cursor/skills/apple-design/SKILL.md) with its web scope (principles and foundations, not iOS or macOS conventions); this design system's own rules win where they differ.
2. **Rule of one.** Every surface has one reader, one idea, one focal point and one next step: pages, sections, heroes, slides, posters, print, documents, emails, messages, share images and app screens. Read [`docs/rule-of-one.md`](docs/rule-of-one.md) before composing or reviewing any of them, write its one-sentence brief first, and run its checklist before calling the work done. When something competes with the one idea, move it to its own section, page or appendix; do not squeeze it in.
3. **Facts.** Prefer [`content/`](content/) first. Use [`docs/handbook.txt`](docs/handbook.txt) as the index, then the section file under [`docs/handbook/`](docs/handbook/), for policies, conflicts, and `[TO CONFIRM]` — not for hero or marketing copy. Never quote handbook §15 (the “wellness retreat” 30-second line) on public pages. Never use “patients” or “wellness retreat” as public product language.
4. **Audience.** [`docs/icp.md`](docs/icp.md) is internal. Do not paste ICP names or competitor paragraphs onto pages. Do not store ICP IDs on content records; ICPs are for strategy, campaigns and CRM only.
5. **Samples.** Files under `ui_kits/website/screens/` illustrate composition. They are not the production site. Production URLs are `/en/…`, not `#/en/…`.
6. **Bundle.** Do not read [`_ds_bundle.js`](_ds_bundle.js) for copy, programmes, or rates. It is components-only.
7. **Leave alone** unless the task needs them: catalog chrome (`catalog.js`, `index.html`), `scraps/`, and `uploads/`.
8. **Nursing and attendants.** Before writing about nursing, nurses, attendants, bystanders or helpers, read [`docs/handbook/glossary.md`](docs/handbook/glossary.md) and follow the naming rules exactly.
9. **Devices, work and amenities.** Before writing about devices, phones, Wi-Fi, work, workstations or amenities, read [`docs/handbook/glossary.md`](docs/handbook/glossary.md). Never add an amenity that is not in the handbook amenities section.
10. **Images.** Before choosing or describing any photograph, read [`assets/photos/README.md`](assets/photos/README.md). It has the public-use status of every frame (Yes, Check, Avoid), a shortlist by job, an index, and known filename errors. For one photo's full entry, search [`assets/photos/CATALOGUE.md`](assets/photos/CATALOGUE.md) for `#### <filename>`. Do not read the whole catalogue, and do not open image files to choose one. For logos, lockups and the rosette pattern, read [`assets/README.md`](assets/README.md). Write alt text from what the catalogue says a frame shows, never from its filename.
11. **Credentials and claims.** Before writing any doctor credential, licence or membership, check the verified credentials list in [`docs/handbook/glossary.md`](docs/handbook/glossary.md). Never add a credential that is not on that list. Never remove or question one that is. A doctor who holds a BNYS, and who is legally entitled to the prefix "Dr.", is written "Dr. [Full Name], BNYS" or "Dr. [Full Name]". Follow the naming rules in that glossary.

Rates, currencies and validity dates are not stored in this design system or its documentation, except the personal attendant charge in that glossary, which is recorded there so the attendant is never described as nursing care. Do not copy that figure onto any other page. Production rates live only in the shantara.life Keystatic tariff. Everywhere else, link to the tariff page and never quote a rate. [`content/tariff.json`](content/tariff.json) holds only the tariff structure (inclusions, supplements, payment terms, cancellation). Nothing the rate sheet marks internal ever enters this folder.

What to take vs leave when consuming this folder: [`docs/use.md`](docs/use.md).
