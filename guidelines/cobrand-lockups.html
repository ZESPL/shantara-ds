Choosing one. Every document uses the endorsed lockup: letterheads, letters, agreements, medical summaries, guest documents, reports and brochures. Use the horizontal version at the page head and, smaller, as the running head on continuation pages. Use the stacked version on covers and footer bands. The co-brand lockup never appears on a document. Keep it for joint material where both organisations stand side by side.
Leave the artwork alone. Shantara is the wordmark only, never the flower stacked with it. The endorsed lockup is always one colour, the Shantara colour in use: Shantara, the hairline and the endorsement line all take it, whether that is Pine Tree, Himalaya, Merino or Gold Crayola. Never mix two colours in it. The endorsed lockup names Hygiene Nature Cure in type only; never put the Hygiene logo in it. Beside Shantara, the endorsement wraps after “A sister concern of”, so “Hygiene Nature Cure” stays whole on the second line. Centred below Shantara, it is one line. Never wrap it anywhere else. In the co-brand lockup Hygiene keeps its own artwork and tones, never Himalaya or Gold Crayola, without its Nature Cure line. Do not retype the endorsement line, change its words or move the hairline. One lockup per page head; the rosette band stays clear of it.
