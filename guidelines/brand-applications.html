Print rules

Always prefer matte over glossy.
Gold foil on envelopes.
Gold foil or raised UV on the icon of the business card.
Use premium papers. No coated stock.

Stationery set

Business card — Himalaya field, flower and name upper-left, contact and QR at the lower right, wordmark on the reverse.
Letterhead — mark and name at the top, contact at the foot, ample white space, pattern only as a foot band.
Envelope & letter cover — Himalaya throughout, contact lower-right, address at the foot or top.
The wordmark is preferred wherever the name should read. The flower is the mark, used where the name already reads. Never stack the flower on the wordmark. Pattern on products is embossed, debossed or single-colour line — never a filled shape.
