The contract

No bounce, no overshoot, no spin, no ease-in on UI. Press is scale(0.96). Hover is a one-step colour, an underline, or (on a website Tile) the photograph scaling to 1.02 inside its frame (--tile-media-scale); the 4px lift is for app-kit cards only. Hover is gated to fine pointers so it never sticks after a tap. Editorial image reveals use --duration-reveal (640ms), one or two per page. Catalog tiles stagger on first paint. Website pages and app tab panels enter with sh-enter-up. Enter uses --ease-out (cubic-bezier(0.23, 1, 0.32, 1)) and never starts from scale(0). prefers-reduced-motion: reduce keeps colour and opacity, drops movement, and freezes the Spinner.

Logo, Icon, Divider, PatternPanelStill. The mark and the rosette do not animate—

Never add a second ambient loop, a bounce easing, a colour flash on press, a rotating spinner, or motion on a keyboard shortcut. Catalog search, tab switches in the app bar, and daily list browsing stay still. If the styling disappeared, the motion should still feel like a breath, not a notification.
