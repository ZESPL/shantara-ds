Tone of imagery

Blooming in nature

Architecture read against weather and planting. Deep greens, warm concrete, monsoon light. Wide, unhurried frames with real air in them. No stock-wellness close-ups and no saturated sunsets.

Privacy rule

Guests are never identifiable. No faces in treatment, no room numbers, no arrival shots. Figures appear at distance, as scale, or not at all. This is a condition of admission, not a style choice.

Every photograph is square-cut (--radius-media: 0) and goes through Media. One photograph per job. Ratio by job: 4:3 listing tiles; 16:9 wide editorial; 4:5 doctor portraits and tall split media; 3:4 tall architecture; 21:9 panorama on desktop only, with a mobileRatio for phones. Page heroes use an explicit height (100svh / 72svh), not a ratio. Type over photos sits inside --scrim-hero, which covers the whole text block, in solid Merino.
