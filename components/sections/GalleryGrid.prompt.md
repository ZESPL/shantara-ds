Photographs of a place in a fixed rhythm of wide and tall frames, with optional captions — no carousel.

```jsx
<Section>
  <GalleryGrid title="The rooms" items={[
    { src: "/assets/photos/room-premium.jpg", alt: "A room with a balcony over the valley", caption: "Premium Room." },
    …
  ]} />
</Section>
```

**Use** on Rooms, Farm & Dining, Amenities & Activities and Hosted visits. Five or ten photographs read best.

**Content.** Frames without guests; real alt text on every photograph; a caption only when it adds something. One photograph per job — no lightbox, no thumbnail strip.

**Responsive.** Phones: one column at 4:3. 760–999px: the first of every five spans the width at 16:9, the rest in pairs. From 1000px (six columns): a wide frame (4 columns, 3:2) beside a tall one that stretches to its height, then three at 4:3; the rhythm repeats.
