# Shantara Premium Presentation Rules

Shantara is a 52-room, doctor-led naturopathy retreat in Kozhikode, Keralam. Its decks are read by investors, shareholders, travel agents, medical tourism professionals, guests and staff. Every deck must feel calm, confident and trustworthy, because the audience is judging a health-related business, not just a beautiful property.

## Core principle

> A premium deck says less, shows better, and proves everything it claims.

Premium feel comes from five things working together:

Story × Photography × Typography × Layout × Evidence

If one is weak, the deck feels cheap. Decoration, effects and extra slides cannot fix a weak one.

## Build order (required)

Work in this order. Do not move to a later step while an earlier one is undecided.

1. Audience and goal (who is reading, what decision or action the deck should produce)
2. Storyline (the sequence of slide headlines, written before any design)
3. Evidence (numbers, sources, proof for each claim)
4. Photography and visuals
5. Typography and layout
6. Colour and detail polish
7. Animation, if any

**Design comes after the story.** If the headlines alone do not tell the full story in order, fix the story before touching layout.

## Know the deck type

Set the goal before writing. Common Shantara deck types:

- **Investor and shareholder decks:** goal is trust and clarity. Lead with performance numbers, then context. Every figure sourced and dated.
- **Monthly scorecard and board reviews:** goal is decisions. Show status against target, what changed, and what needs a decision. No marketing imagery.
- **Agent and medical tourism partner decks:** goal is confident selling. Programmes, who they suit, stay length, published rates, how referrals and commission work, contact details.
- **Guest and programme presentations:** goal is reassurance. What happens each day, who looks after them, what the stay includes.
- **Staff training decks:** goal is behaviour change. One skill or standard per section, real examples, short practice prompts.

## Storyline rules

- One idea per slide.
- Every slide headline is a full sentence that states the point, not a topic label.
  - Good: "Occupancy reached 48% in September, up from 31% in August"
  - Good: "Our 14-day programme is designed for guests managing stress and poor sleep"
  - Avoid: "Occupancy update", "Our programmes", "Overview"
- Headlines are direct and literal. No metaphors, slogans or cryptic phrases.
  - Avoid: "A journey within", "Where healing begins", "The road ahead"
- Reading only the headlines from first slide to last should tell the complete story.
- Open with the conclusion or the ask. Supporting detail follows.
- End with one clear next step: a decision, an action, or a contact.
- Cut any slide that does not move the story forward. Move backup detail to an appendix.

## Photography and visuals

- Use real Shantara photography: property, rooms, treatments, food, landscape, staff. Avoid generic stock wellness images.
- One strong image per slide. Never collage several weak images.
- Full-bleed images for section openers and emotional moments. Keep text off busy parts of the image or place it on a clean area.
- Crop with intent. Keep horizons level and people looking into the frame, not out of it.
- Keep image colour and mood consistent across the deck (similar warmth and brightness).
- Icons only if they add meaning. One consistent thin line style. No clip art, no 3D icons, no emoji.
- Compress images so the file stays easy to email and open on a phone.

## Typography

- Use the same typefaces as the Shantara website design system: one refined serif for headlines, one clean sans-serif for body text and labels.
- Maximum two typefaces in any deck.
- Clear size hierarchy: headline, subhead, body, caption. Keep sizes consistent from slide to slide.
- Body text large enough to read on a phone and on a projector. Avoid tiny footnote-sized text except for sources.
- Left-align body text. Centre only short headlines on opener or quote slides.
- No text in all capitals except very short labels.
- For Arabic versions, use a proper Arabic typeface pairing and mirror the layout right-to-left. Do not just translate the text into an English layout.

## Layout and whitespace

- Fewer elements per slide: usually one headline, one visual, and a short supporting point or number.
- Whitespace is a design choice, not wasted space. Do not fill empty areas.
- Use a consistent grid and consistent margins on every slide.
- Keep the headline in the same position on every content slide.
- Maximum about 30 to 40 words of body text per slide. If more is needed, split the slide or move detail to the appendix.
- No more than 3 or 4 bullets on a slide. Each bullet is a complete, plain sentence.

## Colour and detail

- Use the Shantara brand colours from the website design tokens: two or three earthy base tones plus neutrals.
- One accent colour, used only to highlight the most important thing on a slide.
- Meet WCAG AA contrast for all text, including text on images.
- Logo small and consistent, usually on the cover and closing slides only. Do not stamp it on every slide.
- Slide numbers on content slides for easy reference in meetings.
- No drop shadows, gradients, bevels, glow effects or decorative shapes unless they are part of the brand system.

## Numbers and charts

- Every number has a source and a date or period, shown in small text on the slide.
- Show the comparison that gives the number meaning: against target, last month, or last year.
- One chart per slide, with one message stated in the headline.
- Choose the simplest chart that shows the point: bar for comparisons, line for trends. No pie charts with more than three slices. No 3D charts.
- Remove chart clutter: no heavy gridlines, no legends when direct labels work, no unnecessary decimal places.
- Highlight the key bar or line in the accent colour. Keep the rest neutral.
- Use Indian number formatting (lakh, crore) for Indian audiences and international formatting for Gulf and overseas audiences. Label currency every time (₹, AED, USD).
- Never round in a way that changes the story. If a number is an estimate, label it as an estimate.

## Health and legal wording

- Never say a programme or therapy cures or treats a disease. Use wording like "supports", "designed for guests who" or "may help".
- Any slide about medical conditions, therapies or research is flagged for human review before the deck is shared. India's Drugs and Magic Remedies (Objectionable Advertisements) Act, 1954 applies.
- Agent and partner decks show only Shantara's published rates, taken from the current tariff at the time the deck is made. The brand-deck template stores no rates, currencies or validity dates. Copy amounts exactly; never convert or round them. No discounts or markups presented as available.
- Investor decks do not promise returns. Shareholder entitlements must match the current Shareholder Privilege Program document exactly.
- Use real guest testimonials only with written consent, and never imply a medical outcome.

## Animation and transitions

- Default is no animation.
- If used at all: simple fade only, same speed throughout, for a reason (for example, revealing one bar of a chart after the audience has seen the baseline).
- No flying, bouncing, spinning, zooming or morph effects.
- The deck must work fully as a PDF with no animation, because most people will read it on a phone or laptop without the presenter.

## Do not use

- Topic-label headlines ("Introduction", "Overview", "Update")
- Walls of text or more than 4 bullets
- Stock images of generic spas, stones, bamboo or lotus flowers
- Word art, clip art, 3D charts, emoji
- Multiple accent colours on one slide
- Logo on every slide
- Decorative animation or transitions
- Unsourced numbers

## Review checklist

Before sharing any deck, check:

- [ ] The audience and the single goal are clear.
- [ ] Reading only the headlines tells the full story in order.
- [ ] Every headline is a full, direct, literal sentence.
- [ ] One idea per slide. Backup detail is in the appendix.
- [ ] Only real Shantara photography, consistent in mood.
- [ ] Maximum two typefaces, matching the website design system.
- [ ] Consistent grid, margins and headline position.
- [ ] Every number has a source, a period and a comparison. Currency is labelled.
- [ ] No "cure" or "treat" claims. Health slides are flagged for review.
- [ ] Rates match the current tariff exactly, and entitlements match the current official documents.
- [ ] Text contrast meets WCAG AA, including on images.
- [ ] The deck reads correctly as a PDF with no animation.
- [ ] The final slide states one clear next step.
