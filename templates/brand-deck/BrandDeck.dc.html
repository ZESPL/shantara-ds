A fourteen-night stay

What changes, week by week

The stages are fixed. The therapies inside them are not, and are rewritten at the morning consultation.

01Nights 1–2

Assessment

Blood work, vitals and a full case history.

No therapies until the chart is signed.

02Nights 3–5

Detoxification

Liquid and raw diet, colon hydrotherapy.

Most guests report headaches by night four.

03Nights 6–8

Active treatment

Hydrotherapy, mud and acupuncture daily.

Therapy load reaches its highest point.

04Nights 9–12

Rebuilding

Cooked food returns, portioned by weight.

Yoga therapy moves to strength work.

05Nights 13–14

Handover

Repeat blood work against day one.

A written home protocol and a six-week call.
